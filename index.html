CV

I'm an Assistant Professor at the Department of Linguistics of the Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM).

I work on syntactic and morphological theory, with a focus on argument structure, binding, agreement, clitic doubling, and allomorphy. I specialize in (Modern) Greek and Turkish.

I received my PhD from the University of Pennsylvania in 2023. My dissertation, Voice from syntax to syncretism, can be accessed here.
